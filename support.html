Help with the iTypeFastR keyboard

Contact: questions, problems or ideas? Email us at support@itypefastr.com. Please mention your phone model and system version; the app version is shown at the bottom of What's New in the app.

Turning the keyboard on (iPhone)

  • Open the Settings app.
  • Go to General → Keyboard → Keyboards.
  • Tap Add New Keyboard… and choose iTypeFastR.

Then, in any app, tap the globe key until iTypeFastR appears. iTypeFastR does not need "Allow Full Access".

Subscription

iTypeFastR starts with a free trial, which you start from the app. To manage or cancel your subscription, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions. To restore a purchase on a new phone, open the app and tap Restore Purchases.

Privacy

Nothing you type leaves your device. See the privacy policy.
