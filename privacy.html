Privacy Policy for iTypeFastR

Effective date: September 3, 2026

Summary: iTypeFastR is a custom keyboard extension for iOS and iPadOS. It does not collect, store, transmit, or share any of your personal data, typed content, or usage activity — with us or with any third party. Everything the keyboard does happens entirely on your device.

Who we are

iTypeFastR ("the App", "we", "us") is a third-party keyboard extension for iOS and iPadOS. This policy explains what happens to your data when you install and use iTypeFastR, in line with Apple's App Store guidelines for custom keyboard apps.

Data we collect

We do not collect any data. Specifically:

  • We do not record, log, or transmit anything you type.
  • We do not collect personal information (name, email, contacts, location, etc.).
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • We do not use cookies or any tracking technologies.
  • We do not require or use any account, sign-in, or profile.

"Full Access" permission

iOS keyboard extensions can optionally request "Full Access," which some keyboards use for features like network access, custom sounds, or syncing. iTypeFastR does not request Full Access. The keyboard runs in Apple's standard sandboxed extension environment at all times, which on its own prevents network access and restricts access to the rest of your device.

How the keyboard works

Any text prediction, autocorrection, or typing-assistance features in iTypeFastR are performed entirely on-device, using data and models bundled with or generated locally by the App. Nothing you type is ever sent off your device, to us, or to any third party — including for the purpose of improving the App.

Third parties

We do not share data with third parties because we do not collect any data to share. iTypeFastR does not integrate any third-party SDKs, advertising networks, or analytics services.

Children's privacy

iTypeFastR does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. Because the App collects no data at all, it does not collect data from children either.

Data retention and deletion

Since no data is collected or transmitted, there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored preferences and data from your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example, if a future version of the App adds a feature that involves data collection — we will update this page and revise the effective date above before that feature becomes available.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or the App, contact us at support@itypefastr.com.
